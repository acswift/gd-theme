### To use a colored background around a section

#### Group the elemnts of the section

[[documentation](https://wordpress.com/support/wordpress-editor/blocks/group-block/)]
First, select the block or blocks you wish to group. You can select multiple blocks by clicking and dragging or by holding down the “shift” key and clicking on each of them (they need to be next to each other).

The **block toolbar** will appear.

Click on the **Group** icon (two overlapping squares).
